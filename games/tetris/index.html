MISSION BRIEFING
Objective

Tiles fall one at a time — each is a slice of a real satellite image. Place every tile in the correct column and rotation to reconstruct the full picture.

Edge Hints

Coloured strips on tile edges match the edges of their neighbours in the completed image. Use them to work out where — and which way up — each tile belongs. Difficulty controls how many hints are shown: Easy gives you all four sides, Moderate gives two, Hard gives none.

Controls
← →  Move column    ↑  Rotate tile
↓  Fall faster      SPACE  Hard drop
ESC  Return to menu
Scoring

A correct placement scores 300 pts. Chain 3 or more in a row for a ×2 streak bonus. Wrong column or rotation deducts points and resets your streak.
